For applicable CC BY icons, credit the individual author, link to the source and license, and identify modifications. The upstream contributor notice marks Viscious Speed and Zeromancer as CC0; the official [Infinity](https://game-icons.net/1x1/various-artists/infinity.html) and [Salmon](https://game-icons.net/1x1/various-artists/salmon.html) pages also identify those files as CC0. Check the selected file rather than assuming one license for the entire collection. For badges without a separately named creator, retain the upstream contributor notice.

Fantasy UI Borders' sample-font notice names Aoboshi One under the Open Font License; check font licensing separately if using that font. Golden UI's sample lettering is not a complete font. RPG UI identifies CC0 components [Human Skull by CDmir](https://opengameart.org/content/human-skull-0) and [Scrolls by yd](https://opengameart.org/content/scrolls-0).

The Screaming Brain Studios entry links to its catalogue and selected source pages. No local subset or full-site mirror is distributed here. Source-file URLs, sizes and hashes in `index.json` describe upstream artifacts and do not imply local copies.
