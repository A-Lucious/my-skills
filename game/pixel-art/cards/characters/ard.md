# Full-Body Characters: Aseprite Rough Draft (ard)

Focuses on silhouette negative space, anatomical simplification, hand/weapon exaggeration, and omitting micro-features.

## 1. Radical Facial Simplification
- In full-body sprites (where the head is only 8–14 pixels tall), **DO NOT attempt to draw realistic eyes, nostrils, or lips**.
- Use a simple horizontal 2–3 pixel shadow band for the visor/eyes, or a single highlight pixel on the forehead/helmet crest.

## 2. Negative Space & Silhouette Cleanup
- Clean muddy pixels bridging limbs and torso; ensure clear daylight through negative spaces (under arms, between legs, between cloak and blade).
- Thicken hands, feet, and weapon contours by 1 pixel if they feel too frail at native resolution (pixel landmark exaggeration).

## 3. Major Mass & Equipment Layering
- Consolidate render noise into distinct color planes:
  - Outer Armor / Cloak.
  - Under-tunic / Trousers.
  - Belts, scabbards, and boots.
