# Full-Body Characters: 3D Modeling & Camera Setup (3dac)

Focuses on anatomical proportions, center of gravity, silhouette legibility, and equipment occlusion.

## 1. Proportions & Mannequin Blockout
- **Head-to-Body Ratio:** Establish target proportions (e.g. 3–4 heads for stylized chibi/combat sprites, 6–7 heads for semi-realistic fantasy).
- **Center of Gravity & Stance:** Ensure feet grounding and hip/torso counter-pose establish realistic weight distribution.
- **Head Simplification:** Head is an egg-shaped proxy with a jaw wedge and nose ridge; **STRICTLY DO NOT model facial features** in 3D for full-body characters.

## 2. Primary Silhouette Landmarks
- **Exaggerate Key Silhouette Elements:** Weapons (broadsword, staff), headwear (pointed wizard hat, horned helm), and garments (flowing cloak, shoulder pauldrons) must possess distinct, exaggerated shapes visible in the silhouette.
- **Negative Space:** Maintain clear air gaps between limbs and torso, or between weapons and cloaks.

## 3. Game-Specific Camera Alignment
- Align camera to match in-game view:
  - *Top-Down / 2.5D Isometric:* 30°–45° elevation angle to show both crown/shoulders and grounded feet.
  - *Side-Scroller / Platformer:* Orthographic side or 3/4 perspective.
