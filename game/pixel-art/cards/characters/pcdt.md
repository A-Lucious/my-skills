# Full-Body Characters: Pixel Clusters, Details & Texture (pcdt)

Focuses on edge definition, armor edge highlights, fabric fold rhythms, and limb articulation.

## 1. Outer Contours & Selective Outlining
- Apply dark selective outlines along outer silhouette edges, especially where character limbs overlap or touch contrasting game backgrounds.
- Avoid wrapping the entire sprite in a uniform harsh black cage; use dark tone shades from the corresponding color family.

## 2. Armor Bevels & Weapon Edge Highlights
- Place 1-pixel highlight lines along the leading edges of pauldrons, breastplates, and weapon cutting edges.
- Add small contact shadow clusters beneath belt pouches and armor trim.

## 3. Fabric Fold Rhythms
- Draw broad diagonal fold clusters (2–4 pixels wide) across cloaks or loose pants rather than detailed cross-hatching.
- Maintain broad calm areas without noise.
