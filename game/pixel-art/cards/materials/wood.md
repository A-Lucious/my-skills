# Material Card: Wood

Focuses on grain flow, knot clusters, board seams, and matte reflection.

## 1. Direction & Perspective
- Wood grain clusters must strictly follow the board's planar perspective direction (horizontal along beams, vertical along posts).
- Do not draw cross-grain textures or uniform noise.

## 2. Cluster Structure & Scale
- Form textures primarily with **2–4 pixel short clusters**, interspersed with occasional small 1-pixel knot points and uneven, broken seam lines.
- Total texture pixel coverage should remain sparse (typically **5%–8%** of the visible wood area) to preserve resting space.

## 3. Light Response & Ramps
- Matte finish: broad, soft illuminated zones with minimal sharp specular glints.
- Typically maps to `earth` or warm `leather` tonal ramps in the palette.
