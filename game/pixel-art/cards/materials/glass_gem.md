# Material Card: Glass & Gems

Focuses on internal refraction, faceted planes, transparency, and pin-point glints.

## 1. Transparency & Fluid Volumes
- Glass bottles convey transparency through internal contrast: a dark base/cavity shadow paired with a lighter internal liquid crest.
- Use thin, continuous 1-pixel curved rim highlights along the outer container shoulder to define glass thickness.

## 2. Gem Facets & Specular Glints
- Gemstones rely on sharply contrasting geometric facets (adjacent planes shift abruptly from bright saturation to deep tone).
- Place a single pure-white (255) pin-point glint at the apex facet facing the primary light source.

## 3. Ramps & Color
- Typically maps to `ruby`, `jade`, `teal`, or `emerald` color families.
- Maintain intense core saturation in the translucent body while keeping core shadows rich and luminous.
