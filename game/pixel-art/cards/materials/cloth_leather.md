# Material Card: Cloth & Leather

Focuses on fold rhythms, diffuse vs. satin highlights, thickness, and seam definition.

## 1. Light Response
- **Cloth:** Highly diffuse, zero specular reflection. Light softly rolls across undulating fold planes.
- **Leather:** Semi-matte/satin sheen. Displays gentle, restrained highlights along curved bellies, straps, or stretched bends.

## 2. Fold Rhythms & Clusters
- Draw fabric folds as coherent, directional pixel waves (2–4 pixels wide) following gravity and tension points (elbows, knees, belt pinches).
- Avoid scattered single-pixel noise; leave broad resting areas between major fold lines.

## 3. Thickness & Seams
- Leather possesses palpable physical thickness: represent strap edges with a slightly lighter turned-edge pixel line and dark underside shadow.
- Suggest stitching with subtle, evenly spaced 1-pixel dash patterns using adjacent ramp tones.
