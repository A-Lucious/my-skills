# Material Card: Metal

Focuses on sharp specular highlights, high contrast, steep value falls, and edge bevels.

## 1. Highlight Character
- Metal highlights are **sharp, narrow, and high-contrast** (typically 1-pixel wide along blade edges, armor ridges, or rounded rims).
- Use the brightest step in the active ramp (or off-white/cream) for pure glints.

## 2. Value Hierarchy & Environment Reflection
- Steep value transitions: rapid shift between illuminated faces and deep core shadows with minimal mid-tone blurring.
- Shadow planes often catch subtle cool ambient or ground bounce reflections (e.g. blue-gray or amber bounce).

## 3. Wear & Scratches
- Keep wear restrained: a single notched break in an edge highlight or an isolated dark scratch pixel communicates hardness effectively without dirtying the metal.
- Typically maps to `neutral`, `blue`, or `gold` (for brass/bronze) ramps.
