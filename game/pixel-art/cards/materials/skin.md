# Material Card: Skin

Focuses on organic plane transitions, subsurface warmth, and dithering restraint.

## 1. Value Transitions & Restraint
- Skin requires soft, organic value masses rather than sharp mechanical edges.
- **Strictly prohibit extensive checkerboard dithering across cheeks and forehead**: dithering looks like dirt or aging texture at low resolutions. Use solid, clean pixel clusters.

## 2. Color Temperature & Ramps
- Highlight planes (forehead, cheekbones) use warm, pale skin tones.
- Turning planes shift into saturated warm tones (subsurface peach/salmon nuance).
- Shadow planes use rich warm-brown undertones, avoiding dirty gray or greenish tints.
- Maps exclusively to the `skin` color family.

## 3. Highlighting
- Keep skin highlights subtle and diffuse; pure white (255) is reserved strictly for iris catchlights, never for forehead or cheek shine.
