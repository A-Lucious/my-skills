# Props Specification (Reference Index)

For all prop, equipment, container, and item asset production, inject the corresponding node cards from `cards/props/`:

- **Phase 1 (3D & Camera):** [cards/props/3dac.md](../cards/props/3dac.md) - Assembly, cavity depth, wall thickness, and angles.
- **Phase 2 (Lighting & Render):** [cards/props/lr.md](../cards/props/lr.md) - Cavity shadows, fixture occlusions, and material IDs.
- **Phase 3 (Pixel Shader Preview):** [cards/props/psap.md](../cards/props/psap.md) - Grid budget (64x64 / 32x32), cross-bleed prevention.
- **Phase 4 (Rough Draft):** [cards/props/ard.md](../cards/props/ard.md) - Palette ramp mapping, latch/handle exaggeration.
- **Phase 5 (Details & Texture):** [cards/props/pcdt.md](../cards/props/pcdt.md) - Bevel highlights, seams, and material clusters.
- **Phase 6 (Final Check):** [cards/props/fvc.md](../cards/props/fvc.md) - Slot readability, margins, and palette validation.

Combine with material cards in [cards/materials/](../cards/materials/) as appropriate for the object's constituents.
